About Me

My name is Natnael Samuel. I am a 14-year-old growing up in the vibrant city of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. From an early age, I have been driven by an endless curiosity about how the world around me works. While many people simply use technology, I have always felt a deep pull to look behind the screen, explore what lies beneath the surface, and understand the mechanisms that make modern devices function.

How My Curiosity Began

My journey into technology started with simple everyday exploration. I found myself endlessly fascinated by the inner workings of phones, spending hours diving into settings menus, exploring features, and figuring out how operating systems interact with users. But my true spark for creation came through gaming. As a young kid, I spent countless hours playing Roblox, captivated by its vast virtual worlds. One day, a sudden realization changed everything: I didn't just have to play these games—I could actually build them myself.

Discovering Coding and My Growth

Discovering that Roblox games were created using a scripting language called Lua was a turning point for me. Driven by curiosity, I took on the challenge of learning Lua so I could bring my own game ideas to life. That initial spark quickly turned into a passion for programming. By the time I turned 13, I expanded my horizons beyond game development and began learning Python, HTML, CSS, and other technologies. What started as a simple curiosity about a game transformed into a genuine love for software development and problem-solving through code.

My Favorite Things About Me

My Curiosity: I never settle for just using a tool; I always want to know how it works under the hood. Whether it's navigating through complex device settings or dissecting lines of code, my desire to learn is one of my strongest traits.

My Passion for Coding: Programming gives me the freedom to turn imagination into reality. Building something functional out of raw lines of code is one of the most rewarding feelings I've experienced.

My Love for Gaming: Gaming—especially Roblox—is where my whole technical journey began. It continues to be one of my favorite hobbies, serving as both a source of fun and a constant source of creative inspiration.
