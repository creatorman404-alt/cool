Why Me?

Stepping into the world of technology at a young age isn't just a hobby for me—it is a mindset. As a 14-year-old developer based in Addis Ababa, I bring a rare blend of deep curiosity, self-driven learning, and hands-on experience that sets me apart. Here is why I stand out and what I bring to the table:

Early & Self-Driven Start

My tech journey didn't start in a classroom; it started out of pure curiosity. From diving into phone operating systems as a child to teaching myself Lua on Roblox, and then expanding into Python, HTML, and CSS by age 13, I have consistently taken full initiative over my own learning. I don't wait to be taught—I build, test, break, and fix things until I master them.

A Creator’s Mindset

Where others see games and apps solely as entertainment, I see systems waiting to be understood and improved. My background in gaming on Roblox evolved directly into game logic and game architecture. This transition from consumer to creator gives me a unique problem-solving perspective on how user experiences and backend logic interact.

Adaptability Across Technologies

Starting with Lua gave me a strong foundation in scripting and logic, making my transition into web technologies (HTML, CSS) and versatile backend languages (Python) smooth and natural. I pick up new concepts quickly, adapt to new syntax easily, and enjoy connecting different technologies to build complete projects.

Unstoppable Curiosity & Resilience

Programming is full of bugs, unexpected errors, and complex challenges. My natural urge to explore—the same curiosity that made me dissect phone settings as a kid—drives me to debug patiently and find creative solutions rather than giving up when things get tough.

My Core Strengths at a Glance

Relentless Curiosity: Driven to understand the inner workings of systems, tools, and code.

Fast Learner: Capable of picking up new programming languages and tools independently.

Problem Solver: Comfortable navigating bugs, game logic, and software mechanics.

Focused Passion: Dedicated to turning ideas into real, functional applications
